The K-Class on Another Level





JTBC Lecture Series, K-Class on Another Level was NP XR Stage’s first projects for multiple television episodes. Considering the production format, the XR team not only paid attention to consistent stability but also gradually provided newer technical solutions for more effective delivery of the lecture contents. Virtual Environment recreated from an pre-existing IP was also very interesting. Using virtual camera animation and Unreal Engine blueprints, the technical team consisted of VAD department and VP Stage department worked together to provide an immersive learning experience, not only to the viewer but also the presenters on stage.
