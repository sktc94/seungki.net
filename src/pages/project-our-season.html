iaaa - Our Season
(Official Music Video)





In collaboration with Studio Meta-K, NP XR Stage produced a XR music video for a virtual artist, iaaa. In order to swap the performer’s face with the virtual identity, shots were designed in ways that it opened flexibility in post-production. The entire project was produced by the VP Stage department, starting with environment design, budgets, and logistics, that provided a general learning experience. How the production is relayed onto and from the set was understood through this project.
