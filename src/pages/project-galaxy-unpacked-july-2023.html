Galaxy Unpacked July 2023





Galaxy Unpacked July 2023 was my chance to serve screen producer role at a live event. Branded contents on moving LED screens at the venue were programmed on Disguise Media Server. Communicating between the engine operator lead and the client, I ensured that media files follow an efficient naming and versioning conventions, so that the project stays intact throughout media ingestion. Also, shadowing media playback for a live event was a valuable experience for grasping the key communication and organization skills.
