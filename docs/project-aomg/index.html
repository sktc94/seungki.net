AOMG Online Concert
Above Ordinary 2021





Above Ordinary 2021, the online concert including two entirely-XR segments were produced at NP XR Stage. As the first XR production, the team went through back-to-back calibrations to perfect the quality. Especially calibrating the tight low-angle rail camera was a big challenge. The VP Stage team experimented with multiple XR features provided by Disguise, to battle-test them for the shooting day. Followed by the XR segments, the actual online live concert was also produced at the same venue. This was an opportunity to dive into the necessary works and procedures for a live production.
