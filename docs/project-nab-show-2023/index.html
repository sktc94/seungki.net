NAB Show 2023 ICVFX/XR Debrief


NAB Show 2023 started my flame to expand my journey to the United States to understand the essence of virtual production technology.
Visits to major virtual production studios in Los Angeles and Las Vegas, technical demonstrations of virtual production solution providers, and networking with different service providers from around the globe helped me to form a concrete understanding of the current difficulties and the directions of emerging technology. This was also a time to promote the presence of a XR stage in Korea, leading to continued friendship with studios like XR Studios, Vū Studios, Cuebric, Copilot Virtual Production, and ZeroSpace. Through XR debriefing to the company, our XR department was able to educate the virtual production technology as well as its applications in permanent installation, immersive media experience, and live events.
