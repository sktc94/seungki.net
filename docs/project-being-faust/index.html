Being Faust - Enter Mephisto




Being Faust - Enter Mephisto is NOLGONG’s in-theatre game experience based on Johann Wolfgang von Goethe‘s Faust. NOLGONG is a game experience design studio in Korea, producing game experience throughout cultural and educational sectors. Being Faust - Enter Mephisto was invited by the Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts to perform during the 29th Istanbul Theatre Festival. As my first professional project, all the production documents including the technical riders, contracts, and localization sheets were handled, load-testing was planned at the Museum of Modern and Contemporary Arts in Korea. Arriving in Istanbul, as the project manager, staff accomodation, audience hospitality, and stage management were handled successfully. Although NOLGONG’s creative arts program were on halt during the Covid, the team is in the planning phase of a new NOLGONG Classic to be released.
