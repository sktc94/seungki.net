Hello, I am Tom(Seungki) Chung.


I am a virtual production pipeline specialist, ensuring that the cutting-edge technology facilitates and expands the creative vision. Just as a traditional ceremonial guide puts forth the procedure and the structure to honor meanings in rituals, I oversee the integration of Unreal Engine, tracking technology, and LED volume workflows to stage artistic integrity and compose a seamless, immersive experience.

Coming into the virtual production industry with a background in live events and installation experiences, I am drawn to understanding the core driving forces of the industry, particularly real-time rendering capabilities, emerging AI solutions, and creative endeavors across the globe. I am extending my journey to New York to connect with passionate storytellers, master new tools, and engage directly with global industries. My current focus is on aligning my hands-on skills in Unreal Engine with the rapid pace of its development.

I am currently a reliable technical specialist that thrives in high-pressure, multi-departmental production sets. I believe in the low-level technology that not only sustains but pushes the creative struggles into the next chapter. However, it is the embodiment of the audience that needs to be achieved for a successful media experience. I am driven to refine this fusion of artistry and engineering, ensuring that technology serves the right amount of expansion for human expression.
