2018 Art & Technology Conference




2018 Art & Technology Conference was creatively planned to ponder upon the concept of school and university, in the era of practical knowledge. As the creative director, three major parts were planned out: Special Exhibition, International Showcase, and Seminars. Special Exhibition’s purpose was to deliberately utilize the media equipment provided by school to produce a narrative or an experience that can be told best through the medium. International Showcase was planned to introduce emerging media artists abroad to the students, while introducing our school to the global community. Seminars were consisted of lecture forms that cover both the traditional and the contemporary medium, and discussion forms. The event was successfully concluded,  coordinating 5 teams of staff, inviting 92 artists.
